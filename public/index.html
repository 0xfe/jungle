Click or tap the scene, or press Enter / Return, to show or hide the menu. Double-tap on touchscreens to show it. The menu fades away after 3 seconds unused, except while Settings or Help is open. ? opens this guide with commands expanded, even with the menu hidden. Tap any command to use it; arrow buttons move the view one step. The sound button stays in the top right. Drag to travel; scroll or pinch with two fingers to zoom. Only navigation pauses auto-scroll, which resumes after 3 seconds; menu and sound controls keep it moving. P toggles it off or on. Explore without borders. Hold Shift to travel faster; N visits another landscape, including flowing streams. V visits an exceedingly rare active volcano, with lava, ash and fleeing wildlife. J visits each animal in turn, from deer and swinging monkeys to boas, small snakes, squirrels, boar, zebras, black bears and cubs, beavers, crocodiles, toads, hawks, vultures, fish schools and whales. Whales spend most of their time underwater; stay a while to see one surface.
