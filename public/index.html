Enter / Return shows or hides the menu. Double-tap the scene on touchscreens to show it. ? opens this guide even with the menu hidden. The sound button stays in the top right. Drag to travel; scroll or pinch with two fingers to zoom. Only navigation pauses auto-scroll, which resumes after 3 seconds; menu and sound controls keep it moving. P toggles it off or on. Explore without borders. Hold Shift to travel faster; N visits another landscape, including flowing streams. J visits each animal in turn, from deer and swinging monkeys to boas, small snakes, squirrels, boar, zebras, black bears and cubs, beavers, crocodiles, toads, fish schools and whales. Whales spend most of their time underwater; stay a while to see one surface.
